            🧠 Advanced mechanics

            Plain answer first; open for the details.
            🎰 Shop odds + pool · roll 1-costs at 4–5, 2-costs at 6
            A 3★ needs 9 copies. Two players chasing Ziggs need 18 of 30.
            🔎 Copy finder · can this 3★ still finish, and what will it cost?
            Expected copies per shop = 5 slots × that cost's odds × (copies left ÷ that cost's pool left). Averages: real rolls swing a lot.
            ✋ Holding units + contests · benched units are out of the pool
            • Every copy on any board or bench is out of the pool. A rival holding 4 Ziggs means 4 fewer for you
            • Holding other 1-costs you'll sell later slightly improves your Ziggs odds (fewer other 1-costs in the pool). Selling puts them back
            • Scout rivals' benches before rolling deep. 6+ copies seen elsewhere → think twice (use the Copy finder)
            🎤 Hype + 3★ units · 3★ = 3× the Hype
            • Hype Levels give rewards as you climb, and Kobuko's passive grows with Hype. Teemo unlocks at 900
            🎙️ Seraphine's Indie followers · free units after every fight
            • After each combat, Indie gives a random champion: free reroll fodder, and sometimes a Ziggs or Kobuko
            • When you 3★ any unit, she becomes a Rising Star (bonus notes). Enough 3★s → Superstar (notes at every enemy). Your 3★ Ziggs + Kobuko feed her
            🧬 Duplicators · use them to finish a 3★
            • Lesser Duplicators copy a 3-cost or cheaper unit: Ziggs, Kobuko, Seraphine all qualify
            • Best use: the copy that completes a 2★ or 3★ right now, or your most contested unit. Don't copy a 1★ you might sell
            🛑 When to stop chasing a 3★ · any of these → stop
            • Copies left in the pool are fewer than you need, or barely more
            • The Copy finder says it costs more than your gold minus 10
            • HP under ~40 and the 2★ board already wins some fights: level and add 4-costs instead
